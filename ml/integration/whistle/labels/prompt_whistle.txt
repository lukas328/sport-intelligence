What a whistle looks like: thin, bright, HORIZONTAL lines, usually a stack of 2 to 3 close parallel lines (the pea whistle warble) between about 2.5 and 5 kHz, often with a fainter copy near twice the frequency; 0.1 s to 2 s long; short blasts can come in pairs. Not whistles: vertical streaks (ball bounces, shoe squeaks, claps: broadband, very short), voices and shouting (smeared energy below 2 kHz with wavy harmonics), crowd noise (broad haze), the buzzer/horn (very loud, long, usually lower and with many harmonics: label it with kind "horn").
