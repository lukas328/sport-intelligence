## Controller

The controller for the processing of data on this website and for the accounts on the platform is the operator named in the [legal notice](../impressum). The club is responsible for the videos and data it uploads; Handball Intelligence processes them on its behalf.

## What data we process

- **Account:** name, email address, language, role in the club, login data (password only as a hash).
- **Club data:** teams, roster, matches, events, comments, presentations.
- **Videos** and data derived from them (positions, tracks, scenes).
- **Consents** per person and purpose, with history.
- **Billing data:** billing address, payment status. Card details are processed only by the payment provider.
- **Technical data:** logs for security and troubleshooting.

## Purposes

We process data to provide the platform, compute analyses, issue invoices, ensure security and provide support. Additional purposes (public highlights, public profile, improving detection) only with consent.

## Website and analytics

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party content. In the app, usage statistics are only collected after consent; without consent, no analytics service is loaded. The support chat is only loaded when you open it.

## Recipients

We use service providers for hosting, sending emails, payments, error monitoring and (optionally) support chat and analytics. They process data only on our behalf. The specific providers and storage locations will be listed here before launch.

## Retention

- Original videos: 90 days, after which the compressed analysis version remains.
- Account and club data: until deleted by you or the club.
- Invoices: as long as required by law.

## Your rights

You can request information, download your data, have it corrected and deleted, and withdraw consents at any time. Most of this can be done directly in the app under "Settings, Privacy" and "Consents". For anything else, write to us. You can also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, with your supervisory authority.

## Security

Encrypted transmission, separate data per club at database level, two-factor sign-in, regular backups and security checks.
