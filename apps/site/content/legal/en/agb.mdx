## 1. Scope

These Terms and conditions apply to the use of the Handball AI platform by clubs, teams and their members. The contracting party is the club that opens an account.

## 2. Services

Handball AI provides a web-based platform for video analysis, statistics and collaboration in handball. The scope depends on the plan chosen on the pricing page. Automatic analyses are based on image recognition methods; their results may be incorrect and are labelled as automatically detected or estimated.

## 3. Account and roles

The club decides who gets access and with which role, and is responsible for managing access. Login details must be treated as confidential.

## 4. Video recordings

With every upload, the club confirms that it is allowed to make and upload the recording. In particular:

- the hall rules permit filming,
- the opponent and those present have been informed about the recording (for example with a notice),
- the necessary consents have been obtained for the club's own players who are filmed, and for minors those of their parents.

Public content (for example public highlights) only shows people who have consented for this purpose.

## 5. Data and rights

Videos and data remain the property of the club or of the persons concerned. Handball AI uses them only to provide the service. Use for improving detection only takes place with the express consent of the person concerned (purpose "Improving detection"), is switched off by default and, for minors, never happens without parental consent.

When changing clubs, players can export their profile and their statistics; videos stay with the club.

## 6. Prices and payment

The prices on the pricing page at the time of conclusion apply. Monthly subscriptions are paid by card, annual subscriptions by QR-bill, payable within 30 days. In the event of late payment, a reminder and a formal reminder follow; after that, access may be restricted to the Free plan.

## 7. Term and cancellation

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at the end of the current month, annual subscriptions at the end of the term. After the end, the club remains on the Free plan. The club can delete its account with all data at any time.

## 8. Retention

Original videos are deleted 90 days after upload; the compressed analysis version is kept until the club deletes it. The club is notified seven days in advance.

## 9. Liability

Handball AI is only liable for damage in the case of intent or gross negligence, to the extent permitted by law. The club is responsible for decisions made on the basis of automatic analyses.

## 10. Changes

Changes to these Terms and conditions are announced by email at least 30 days before they take effect.

## 11. Governing law and jurisdiction

Swiss law applies. The place of jurisdiction is the registered office of the operator.
