## Why the spot matters more than the camera

In a good match video you can always see all twelve court players, both goal areas and the lines. Any phone can do that if it stands in the right place. If it is too low or in a corner, even an expensive camera does not help: players hide each other, and half of the court is far away.

## Where does the camera go?

**Level with the halfway line, on the long side of the court.** From there both goals are equally far away and you never need to pan. A few metres off the halfway line works too, but the middle is best.

Good spots are the stand, a gallery, a railing on the first floor or a tall tripod behind the bench. Less good are the corners and behind the goal.

## How high should the phone be?

**As high as possible, from about 3 metres.** The higher the camera, the less the players hide each other and the better you see the gaps in the defence. At eye level you mostly see the backs of the nearest players.

Without a stand, a tall tripod helps. Put it where nobody trips over it and no emergency exit is blocked, and ask the caretaker first.

How much space a match needs depends on the phone. Many phones show in the camera settings how much space one minute of video takes. At 1080p it is roughly 40 to 100 MB a minute, depending on the setting, so a match with half-time quickly takes 5 to 9 GB.

## Which settings?

- **Resolution:** 1080p (Full HD). 4K adds little for analysis and fills the storage four times as fast.
- **Frames per second:** 50 or 60 if the phone can do it. Fast shots are then less blurred. 25 or 30 also work.
- **Airplane mode on:** otherwise a call can stop the recording.
- **Screen lock off,** brightness down. That saves battery.
- **No zoom, no portrait.**

## Am I allowed to film?

Before the match, tell the opponent, the referees and the hall that you are filming, and put a notice on the door. Inform your own team beforehand and ideally ask for their consent, for children their parents' consent. How that works: [Filming in a club: consent, children, retention](video-im-verein-einwilligung). The notice to print is on the page [Filming](../aufnahme).
