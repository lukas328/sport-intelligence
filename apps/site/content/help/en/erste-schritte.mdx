From your account to your first analysis.

## 1. Account and team

Sign up with your email address. Then you search for your team. If the app finds it at the federation, it shows you what it found: name, league, roster and shirt colour. If everything is right, tap "That's right, continue". If it finds nothing, you enter the name and colour yourself.

## 2. First video

In "Matches", choose the match and upload the video. How to film well is explained under [Filming a match](aufnahme). No video yet? Then watch the demo match first.

## 3. No need to wait

The analysis runs by itself. You need not stay: you get an e-mail when the analysis is done.

## 4. Check

Where the analysis is unsure, it asks you, for example "Goal or not?" or "Is the court right?". In the test with a five-minute match clip: 22 questions with shot spot in six to nine minutes for nine goals. For a whole match we have not measured it yet. You can stop at any time.

When you confirm a goal, the app asks right away "From where?". You see the attacked half as it lies in the video, with the goal on the left or the right, and tap once where the shot came from: left wing, left back, centre back, right back, right wing or pivot. Beside it are "Seven-metre throw" and "Fast break", and "Don't know" if you cannot see it. The clip keeps playing in a loop meanwhile. If the court is not confirmed yet, the goal is at the top, and left and right are as the attack sees them. The three things come from these answers.

## 5. Watch and share

You see the scenes, the numbers and, with enough goals, the three things. Pick what you want to show the team with "Put together for the team". Then send the link to the group chat. See [Reading the analysis](analyse-lesen).
