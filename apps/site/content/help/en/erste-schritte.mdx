From your account to your first analysis.

## 1. Account and team

Sign up with your email address. Then you search for your team. Teams in Switzerland are found in the handball.ch data, and the app shows you what it found: name, league, roster and shirt colour. If everything is right, tap "That's right, continue". Everyone else sets up their team by hand in a minute, with name and colour.

## 2. First video

In "Matches", choose the match and upload the video. How to film well is explained under [Filming a match](aufnahme). No video yet? Then watch the demo match first.

## 3. No need to wait

The analysis runs by itself. You need not stay: at the top of the app you see how far it is and roughly how long it still takes, and the bell tells you when it is done.

## 4. Check

Where the analysis is unsure, it asks you, for example "Goal or no goal?" or "Is the court right?". There are at most ten questions per match, and you are usually through in a few minutes. You can stop at any time, and with "Done for this match" you put all the others aside: they wait under "Check more", and the bell stays quiet.

You confirm the court with "Looks right". You only move the corners when it does not fit. If the camera stands the same way in the same hall at the next match, the analysis takes the court over from an earlier video, and a glance is enough.

When you confirm a goal, the app offers "From where?" right away, if you like. You see the attacked half as it lies in the video, with the goal on the left or the right, and tap once where the shot came from: left wing, left back, centre back, right back, right wing or pivot. Beside it are "Seven-metre throw", "Fast break" and "Skip", all the same size: "Skip" goes on to the next question without a shot spot. The clip keeps playing in a loop meanwhile. If the court is not confirmed yet, the goal is at the top, and left and right are as the attack sees them. The three things come from the shot spots; if one is missing, you add it later if you like.

## 5. Watch and share

You see the scenes, the numbers and, with enough goals, the three things. Pick what you want to show the team with "Put together for the team". Then send the link to the group chat. See [Reading the analysis](analyse-lesen).
