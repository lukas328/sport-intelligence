From your account to your first three things.

## 1. Account and team

Sign up with your email address. Then you search for your team. If the app finds it at the federation, it shows you what it found: name, league, roster and shirt colour. If everything is right, tap "That's right, continue". If it finds nothing, you enter the name and colour yourself.

## 2. First video

In "Matches", choose the match and upload the video. How to film well is explained under [Filming a match](aufnahme). No video yet? Then watch the demo match first.

## 3. No need to wait

The analysis runs by itself. You need not stay: you get an e-mail when the three things are ready.

## 4. Check

Where the analysis is unsure, it asks you, for example "Who is this?" or "Is the court right?". This usually takes a few minutes. You can stop at any time.

## 5. Watch and share

You see the three things, the scenes and the numbers. Pick what you want to show the team with "Put together for the team". Then send the link to the group chat. See [Reading the analysis](analyse-lesen).
