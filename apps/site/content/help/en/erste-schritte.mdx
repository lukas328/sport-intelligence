From sign-up to your first analysis in five steps.

## 1. Create a club

Sign up with your email address. The assistant asks for the club name, logo and club colour. The colour later appears in reports and in the player.

## 2. Team and roster

Create your team (for example "Men 1" or "U15 Girls") and choose the match format. You add the roster one by one or as a CSV file. More on this under [Managing the roster](kader).

## 3. Invite people

Invite coaching staff and players by link. Each person gets a role that determines what they see. See [Roles and permissions](rollen-und-rechte).

## 4. Upload your first match

Create a match, enter the shirt colours and upload the video. The analysis starts automatically as soon as the upload is complete. Camera tips can be found under [Filming a match](aufnahme).

## 5. Read the analysis

After processing you see events, key figures and scenes. What the figures mean is explained under [Reading the analysis](analyse-lesen).

The checklist on the dashboard shows you at any time which steps are still open.
