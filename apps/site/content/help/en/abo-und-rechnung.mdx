Everything about plans, the trial and payment.

## Plans

Free, Team and Club. Current prices and limits are on the [pricing page](../preise). You can try Team and Club free for 30 days, without a payment method.

## Paying

- **Monthly by card**
- **Annually by invoice:** QR-bill, payable within 30 days. The annual subscription costs 10 monthly fees.

The invoice is sent by email to the club's billing address, for example to the treasurer.

## Vouchers

Associations and partners issue vouchers. You enter the code when you subscribe.

## Cancelling

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled to the end of the month, annual subscriptions to the end of the term. Afterwards the club stays on the Free plan; data is kept, features above the Free plan are locked.

## Failed analyses

An analysis that does not finish because of an error does not count towards your quota.
