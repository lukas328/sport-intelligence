All about plans, the trial and paying.

## Plans

"Free", "Team" and "Club". The current prices are on the [Pricing](../preise) page. They are not final yet. According to the plans, Team and Club can be tried free for 30 days.

## Paying

- **Monthly by card.**
- **Yearly by QR bill**, payable within 30 days. The yearly plan costs 10 monthly fees.

The bill is sent by email to the club's billing address, for example to the treasurer.

## Vouchers

Federations and partners issue vouchers. You enter the code when you sign up for a plan.

## Cancelling

Monthly plans end at the end of the month, yearly plans at the end of the term. After that your team stays on the "Free" plan. Matches and scenes are kept, the automatic analysis is locked.

## Analyses that fail

An analysis that does not finish because of an error does not count.
