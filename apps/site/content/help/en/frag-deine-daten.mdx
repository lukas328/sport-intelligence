Under "Ask" in the team you ask questions about your matches in plain language, for example "How often do we score after a timeout?" or "Show all goals conceded via the right wing in the last 5 matches".

## How the answers are made

The assistant does not calculate anything itself. It chooses predefined queries over your tagged events, for example "shooting percentage with filter" or "compare two periods", and phrases the answer from them. Every figure in the answer must come from one of these queries; otherwise it is not shown. Under "How this answer was made" you can see each query.

## What a good answer contains

- the figure with its sample (how many shots or events are behind it),
- scenes in the video, if the events have a video time,
- for comparisons, a note that it is an observation and not proof.

## What it does not answer

Questions for which there is no data (running speed, spectators, predictions) and questions about people by name. The assistant only knows players by their shirt number. In these cases it says so and suggests a question that can be answered.

## Who can use it

Coaching staff, analysts, and goalkeeping and fitness coaches. Conversations are visible only to you. Each club has a monthly allowance; once it is used up, it continues the following month.
