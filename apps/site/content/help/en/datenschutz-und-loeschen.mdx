Your data belongs to you and your club.

## Where the data is stored

The hosting provider will be decided before launch; the goal is a data centre in Switzerland. The exact storage location will then be stated in the privacy policy. Processing follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (Swiss FADP) and the GDPR.

## Downloading your data

In the account menu, under "Settings", "Data and account" you can download all the data we have stored about you (JSON).

## Deleting your account

Your account, your sessions and your memberships are deleted. Comments are kept without your name.

## Deleting the club

Club admins can delete the entire club: all teams, matches, videos and data. This is permanent.

## Videos

Original videos are deleted after 90 days; the compressed analysis version is kept until you delete it or the match.

## Analytics

Usage statistics are only collected with your consent (notice on your first visit). Details are in the [privacy policy](../datenschutz).
