The videos and data belong to your club.

## Where the data is stored

The app and its database run on servers in Switzerland. The videos are kept in storage in the EU. We name the providers before launch in the [privacy policy](../datenschutz). The Swiss Data Protection Act applies and, where needed, the GDPR.

## Who sees what

Only the team's coaching staff sees the video. Others only see what you share with a link. Shared links expire and can be blocked at any time.

## Downloading your data

In your account under "My data" you can download all data stored about you.

## Deleting your account

Your account, your logins and your memberships are deleted. Notes you have shared stay, without your name.

## Deleting the club

Club admins can delete the whole club: all teams, matches, videos and data. This is final.

## Videos

Original videos are deleted after 90 days. A smaller version for the scenes stays until you delete it or the match.
