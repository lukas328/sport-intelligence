The scouting report summarises what the data says about the next opponent.

## When it is created

Automatically 72 hours before the match, as soon as data on the opponent is available: from your own matches against them or from videos of their matches that you have uploaded. You can also create it manually at any time.

## Contents

- The opponent's shot charts and goal zones
- Notable players by shirt number
- Turnovers and tempo
- Goalkeeper: where he saves, where he does not

The observations are statements from the data, not instructions. Each one states the sample and links to the clips. If there are only a few matches, the report points this out.

## Sharing

The report can be downloaded as PDF and added to a presentation.
