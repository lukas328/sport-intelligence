Every person in the club has a role per team. The role determines what they may see and change.

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| Club admin | Manage club, subscription, teams and members |
| Coach | Edit matches, videos, events, presentations and plays |
| Analyst | Like Coach, without team management |
| Goalkeeper coach, Athletic coach | Read analyses, scenes and comments for their area |
| Team manager | Organise matches and roster, no analysis work |
| Player, Goalkeeper | Own scenes, tasks and shared content |
| Parent | Only their own child's content and the child's consents |
| Guest | Only read shared content |

## Changing a role

Club admins change roles in the settings under "Members". Invitations already include the role; it can be adjusted later.

## Handing over admin

Anyone leaving the club transfers the admin role to another person. A club always has at least one admin.

## Two-factor sign-in

For admins and coaching staff we recommend signing in with an authenticator app. Keep the recovery codes in a safe place.
