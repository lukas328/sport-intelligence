Every person has one of four roles per team. The role determines what they may see and change.

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| Coach | Sees and edits everything, including club, teams and subscription. Is club admin. |
| Assistant coach | Sees and edits everything in the team: videos, scenes, squad, invitations. Not club and subscription. |
| Player | Sees everything in the team, marks scenes, builds collections, shares links and uploads videos. Deletes nothing, invites nobody. |
| Parents | See only their own child's dates and consents, no videos. |

Anyone without an account sees only what you share by link.

## Changing a role

Coaches change roles under "Club" › "Teams", next to the person. Invitations already include the role; it can be adjusted later.

## Handing over admin

Anyone leaving the club transfers the admin role to another person. A club always has at least one admin.

## Two-factor sign-in

For coaches and assistant coaches we recommend signing in with an authenticator app. Keep the recovery codes in a safe place.
