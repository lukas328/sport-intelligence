Under "My scenes", players see everything that concerns them.

## What appears

- Scenes you are involved in (once your track has been assigned to you)
- Clips the coaching staff send you, with a comment
- Tasks, for example "Watch your shots from the left"

## Commenting

You can ask questions directly on the clip. The coaching staff see them in the team feed.

## Privacy

You only see your own scenes and content shared with the whole team. Other players do not see your personal clips.
