The Statistics matrix is a toolkit for your own reports.

## Structure

- **Rows:** player (number), position, play, match, opponent, half, match phase in 10-minute segments, shot zone or shot type.
- **Columns:** up to eight key figures, for example goals, shooting percentage, saves, turnovers or xG.
- **Filters:** team (us or opponent), recent matches, half.

A click on a cell shows the scenes behind it, provided the events have a video time.

## Templates

Save a setup as a template, for example "Shooters per half". Templates apply to the whole team.

## Export

The matrix can be downloaded as CSV or Excel. Under "Exchange data" in the match there is also export as Sportscode XML and as CSV of all events, as well as import of Sportscode XML: each instance becomes a clip, and code and labels become separate tags. Imported tags do not change any key figures.
