Videos show people. That is why the app asks for consent per person and purpose.

## The purposes

- **Video analysis in the team:** the team may analyse match videos and recognise you in them.
- **Highlights within the club:** scenes with you may appear in highlights that only the club sees.
- **Public highlights:** scenes with you may be shown publicly.
- **Public profile:** a profile with selected scenes and figures may be public.
- **Improving detection:** anonymised excerpts may help to improve detection.

Each purpose can be chosen individually and withdrawn at any time. Without consent for public purposes, a person does not appear in public content.

## Children and young people

For under-16s, the parents decide. They are invited as Parent, linked to the child and only see the child's content.

## Where you manage them

In the account menu (your initials at the bottom left, top right on a phone), under "Consents" you see your own and, as a Parent, those of your child. Coaches see the status for their team but cannot change it for others.
