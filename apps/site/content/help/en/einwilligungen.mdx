Videos show people. That is why the club asks every player.

## The link for the team

Under "Team" you find a link for the consents. Send it to the team chat. Whoever opens it needs no account: search your name, say yes or no to each point, done.

## The points

- **Video analysis in the team:** the coaching staff may analyse the match videos and recognise the person in them.
- **Highlights in the club:** scenes with the person may appear in highlights that only the club sees.
- **Public highlights:** scenes with the person may be shown publicly.
- **Improving detection:** clips may help to improve the analysis.

Each point can be chosen separately and changed at any time. Anyone who says no to highlights does not appear in highlights.

## Children and young people

The law has no fixed age; it depends on capacity of judgement. In the app the parents decide up to 16, because that is simple and cautious.

## Where you see the status

Under "Team" each person shows whether consent is missing. Only the person themselves can change it, or a parent for children.
