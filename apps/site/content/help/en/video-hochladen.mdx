How your match video gets into the app.

## Uploading

Open the match under "Matches" and choose "Upload video". Use the file as the phone saved it, usually MP4 or MOV.

- **Interrupted?** Choose the same file again. The upload continues where it stopped.
- **Mobile data:** Large files are best uploaded on Wi-Fi. The app warns you.
- **Several files:** for example one per half. Upload them one after the other.
- **By link:** You can also paste a direct download link (https), for example from a camera platform.

## After uploading

The analysis starts by itself. You see which step is running, and you need not stay. If an analysis fails, it does not count, and the app tells you what you can do.

## Match report

If there is a match report (for example from the federation), the app takes the goals, the scorers and the penalties from it. Then the result and the course of the match are right, even without video.

## How long videos are kept

The original video is deleted after 90 days. A smaller version for the scenes stays. You get a message seven days before.
