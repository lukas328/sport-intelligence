This is how your match video gets into the app.

## Uploading

Open the match and drag the video file into the upload area or select it. Common formats such as MP4 and MOV are supported. Large files are uploaded in parts.

- **Pause and resume:** If the connection drops, select the same file again. The upload continues where it left off.
- **Mobile data:** The app warns you before large uploads over mobile data and offers to upload later over Wi-Fi.
- **By link:** You can also paste a direct download link (https), for example from a camera platform.

## Camera role

Choose the role for each file: main camera, goal camera left, goal camera right or additional camera. You upload several files from one camera (for example first and second half) one after another.

## After the upload

Processing starts automatically. You see the progress for each step and get a notification as soon as the analysis is finished. If an analysis fails, it does not count towards your quota and the app tells you what you can do.

## Retention

The original video is deleted after 90 days; the compressed analysis version is kept. You get a message seven days beforehand.
