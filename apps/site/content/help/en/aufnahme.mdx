The quality of the analysis depends above all on the recording. With these rules it works even with a phone.

## Setting up the camera

- **One fixed camera**, not handheld and no zooming.
- **Elevated**, from about 3 metres: stand, gallery or a tripod with an extension.
- **Level with the centre line**, so both goals are equally visible.
- **The whole court in the frame**, including both goal areas and sidelines.

## Settings

- At least 1080p, ideally 50 frames per second.
- Phone in landscape, flight mode on, screen lock off.
- Enough storage and a power bank: a match takes around 90 minutes including breaks.

## Additional cameras

Goal cameras to the left and right behind the goal are optional. They are synchronised automatically via the throw-off and are particularly helpful for goalkeeper analysis.

## Notice in the hall

Let the opponent and the hall know that you are filming. In the app you will find a template for a notice ("This match is being filmed for analysis purposes"). By uploading, the club confirms that it is allowed to make the recording.

## What does not work well

Panning cameras, strong backlight, recordings from behind the goal as the only camera and footage where part of the court is missing. In these cases the analysis detects less and marks uncertain events.
