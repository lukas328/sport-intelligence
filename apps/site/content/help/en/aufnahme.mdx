A phone is enough. Where it stands matters more than the camera.

## Setting up

- **Level with the centre line**, so both goals are equally easy to see.
- **As high as possible**, from about 3 metres: stands, gallery or a tall tripod.
- **Wide angle (0.5× or "Ultra Wide")**, so both goals and goal areas are fully in the picture. If the two near corners are missing, that is fine. If it does not fit: higher or further back.
- **Fixed**: do not pan and do not zoom.

## Settings

- Full HD (1080p), if possible 50 frames per second.
- Phone sideways, flight mode on, screen lock off.
- A power bank and at least 10 GB of free storage: a match with breaks lasts about 90 minutes.
- {MULTI_FILE ? "Best let the recording run through half time. If you stopped, upload all files to the same match." : "Keep filming through half time. If you stop, a second file is made, and the second half is not analysed."}

## What works less well

- A camera that pans: it works, but the court is harder to detect. The app then asks you if the court is right. Without a confirmed court there are no running paths.
- Strong backlight through windows.
- Filming only from behind the goal, or a picture where part of the court is missing.

## Notice in the hall

Tell the opponent and the hall that you are filming. You can find a notice to print on the [Filming](../aufnahme) page. By uploading, the club confirms that it may make the recording.
