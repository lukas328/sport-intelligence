The scene search finds moments across all matches.

## Filtering

Combine filters: match or season, event type (shot, goal, turnover, penalty), outcome, zone, player, match phase and your own tags. The results list shows matching clips with a thumbnail.

## Your own tags

Your own tags complement the automatic events, for example "7 against 6" or "Cross left". You set them in the player at a point or over a range. Your own tags do not change any key figures.

## Reusing

You can

- save selected results as a playlist and share it with the team,
- add them to a presentation,
- send them to players as a task.
