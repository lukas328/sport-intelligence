Under "Scenes" you find moments across all matches.

## Search

Type what you are looking for in the search field, for example "goal conceded right wing last 3 matches". What the app recognises appears as a filter. More filters are suggested with a dashed outline.

## The answer

Above the scenes is the answer with its basis, for example "24 of 66 goals conceded via the right wing". With "Play all" you watch them one after the other.

## Using it further

- **Add to a collection:** put scenes together for the team talk and share the link.
- **As a table:** the same answer as numbers.

Each scene shows the build-up to the goal on a small court and what was read, for example "Number 5 read" or "Shooting position estimated".
