On the tactics board you draw plays in phases.

## Creating a play

Start with a template (for example 6-0 defence or cross) or with an empty court. Place players and the ball, draw running paths and passes and add phases. The animation plays the phases one after another.

## Controls

Arrow keys move a selected element by 0.5 m, with Shift by 2 m. Delete removes it.

## From the video

In the player you take over the positions from the video analysis of a moment as the first phase. The positions are estimates from a single camera.

## Using plays

You can assign plays to the team as a task and include them in presentations.
