After processing, the match page shows video, events and key figures side by side.

## Events

The list contains shots, goals, turnovers, penalties and timeouts. A click jumps to that point in the video. Events have a source:

- **Automatically detected:** from the video analysis. Uncertain events are marked and should be checked briefly.
- **Tagged:** by you or your team, for example live courtside.
- **Corrected:** you can change any event. Corrections feed into the key figures.

## Key figures

Shooting percentage, save percentage, turnovers, shot charts by zone and the course of the match. **xG** (expected goals) estimates how many goals would be expected on average from the shooting positions. This is an estimate and is shown as such.

## Insights

Under "Insights" you will find notable patterns compared with the other matches of the season. Only statements with a sufficiently large sample appear; each one links to the related scenes.

## Limits

Positions and running paths from a single camera are estimates. Values based on heuristics are labelled as such. If something is wrong, correct it or report it via "Feedback".
