What you see after the analysis and where it comes from.

## The three things

For each match, three sentences with a number and its basis, for example "9 of 18 goals conceded via the right wing". Below them, the build-ups to these goals on half a court, and the matching scenes.

- **Solid line:** build-up to the shooting position, confirmed by you.
- **Dashed line:** estimated.
- **Thin line:** to the goal. This is not a shot path: the analysis does not track the ball.

## The tactics camera

The scene from above: players as dots, with their paths over the last two seconds. Shirt numbers only appear where they were read. The tactics camera needs a court that was detected or confirmed by you.

## The numbers

Each number shows where it comes from:

- **Match report:** goals, 7 m, penalties, turnovers.
- **Marked by you:** shots, saves, missed shots. Without marking, the field stays empty instead of showing a zero.
- **From the video, estimated:** running paths, shooting positions, fast breaks.

## What it cannot do yet

Track the ball. Detect saves and missed shots by itself. Reliably detect goals by itself: the analysis suggests moments when a goal may have been scored, and you confirm them in Check. It does not read the hall's scoreboard yet. If something is wrong, correct it in Check or report it via "Help".
