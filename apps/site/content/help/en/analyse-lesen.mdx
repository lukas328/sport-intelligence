What you see after the analysis and where it comes from.

## The patterns

For each match, up to three sentences with a number and its basis. They come from the goals with a shot spot, from three goals from the same zone; with few goals there are fewer than three. An example: "11 of 29 goals conceded via the right wing". Below them, the build-ups to these goals on half a court, and the matching scenes.

- **Solid line:** build-up to the shooting position, confirmed by you.
- **Dashed line:** not confirmed by you yet.
- **Thin line:** to the goal.

A pattern appears as soon as three goals come from the same spot, and it shows right away, even if there is only one so far. For that the goals with a shot spot count: the app suggests it in "Check" after a goal, and you confirm it with one tap, if you like. Where it is missing, for example because you skipped it or for goals from the match report, the place of the patterns says for how many goals, and "Add" takes you through the scenes goal by goal. You can change a shot spot in the list "Answered" and at every scene with "Change shot spot".

## The tactics camera

The scene from above: players as dots, with their paths over the last two seconds, the ball with whoever has it. Shirt numbers appear where they were read in the picture. The tactics camera needs a court that was detected or confirmed by you.

## The numbers

Each number shows where it comes from:

- **Match report:** goals, 7 m, penalties, turnovers.
- **Marked by you:** shots, saves, missed shots. Without marking, the field stays empty instead of showing a zero.
- **Confirmed by you:** the shot spot of the goals where you confirmed or tapped it. A suggestion you have not confirmed shows as "estimated" and does not count. Where it is missing, it says "No shot spot".
- **From the video:** running paths and positions.

## What you do

The analysis suggests the moments when a goal was scored and asks you in Check where it is unsure: at most ten questions per match. You confirm the suggested shot spot with one tap if you like; you mark saves and missed shots if you want them. If something is wrong, correct it in Check or report it via "Help".
