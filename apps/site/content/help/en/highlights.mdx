After the analysis, the app automatically compiles highlights.

## How they are made

From reliably detected scenes: goals, saves and fast breaks. Uncertain scenes are not included. If there are too few reliable scenes, no highlight video is created.

## Variants

- Match highlights for the team
- Personal highlights per player (once the track has been assigned)
- Portrait format for social media, with a spotlight on the person

## Consents

Highlights respect the [Consents](einwilligungen): anyone who has not agreed does not appear in highlights for the club or the public. Public links show shirt numbers instead of names.

## Sharing

Highlights can be downloaded as MP4 or shared by link. You can revoke links at any time.
