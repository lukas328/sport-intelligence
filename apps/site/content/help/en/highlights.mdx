After the analysis, the app puts together highlights by itself.

## How they are made

From the goals and scenes where the analysis is sure. Unsure scenes are left out. If there are too few sure scenes, there is no highlight video.

## Landscape and portrait

Highlights come in landscape for the screen and portrait for the phone.

## Consents

Anyone who said no to highlights does not appear in them. See [Consents](einwilligungen).

## Share

You share highlights by link, for example in the team chat. You can block the link at any time.
