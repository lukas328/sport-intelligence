The roster is the basis for per-person statistics and for consents.

## Adding players

Add people one by one or import a CSV file with the columns first name, last name, number, position, throwing hand and year of birth. Semicolons and commas both work as separators.

## Shirt numbers

Numbers are unique per team. If someone changes their number, earlier matches stay assigned to the right person.

## Matching in video

The analysis detects tracks on the court, but not automatically who is who. Assign tracks of your own team to a person once; the assignment is kept even after a new analysis.

## Opposing players

By default, opponents are only listed by shirt number. Only enter a name from a public source, for example from the official match report.

## Minors

For players under 16, the parents manage the consents. Invite them as Parent and link them to the child. See [Consents](einwilligungen).
