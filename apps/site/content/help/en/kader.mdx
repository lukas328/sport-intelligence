Under "Team" you find your roster: for numbers, names and consents.

## Where the roster comes from

If your team is linked to the federation, the app takes the roster from there. Otherwise you add players yourself, with name, number and position.

## What is missing

If something is missing for a person, it shows in yellow next to them: "Consent missing" or "Number missing".

## Who is who in the video

The analysis reads shirt numbers where they are easy to see and links them to the roster. If it is not sure who someone is, it asks you in Check: "Who is this?". Your answer is kept, even in a new analysis.

## Opposing players

The app lists opponents by shirt number only. Names come at most from the official match report.

## Coaching staff

Under "Coaching staff" you invite other coaches, for example someone who films or checks.
