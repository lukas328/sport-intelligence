For the video session, you put clips, plays, text and figures together into a presentation.

## Creating

Under "Presentations" you create a new one. Clips come from the [scene search](tags-und-filter); you add text, figure cards and plays directly in the editor. You change the order by dragging.

## Per clip

- Slow motion for a section
- Pause points with drawings (arrows, circles, text, spotlight on a person)
- Notes only for you

## Presenting

Presentation mode runs full screen. With your phone as a remote (scan the QR code, same account) you move forward and see your notes.

## Offline

A presentation can be downloaded in advance for the changing room and then runs without internet.

## Export

Exported as MP4 with burned-in drawings, or as PDF with still images. The presentation can be shared with the team.
