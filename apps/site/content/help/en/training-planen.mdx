Plan training sessions directly in the app, record attendance and connect insights from matches with training.

## Planning a session

Under "Training", "Planning" you create a session: date, start time and drills from the drill library with duration and note. You change the order with the arrows. Printed as PDF, you can take the plan with you to the gym.

## Attendance

Players accept or decline training sessions and matches. After the session, the coaching staff record who was present, excused, injured or late. Reasons are optional; a diagnosis does not belong there.

## Subscribing to the calendar

Under "Subscribe to calendar" you get a link that you subscribe to in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. It contains the team's matches and training sessions. Anyone with the link can see the dates; you can generate a new one at any time.

## From problem to training

For an insight from a match (for example "low shooting percentage from the right wing") you choose "Train this". With one click a training session with suitable drills, a playlist with the supporting scenes and, if you wish, a task for the team are created.

The app then tracks the relevant key figure over the next matches and shows "before and after". This is an observation, not proof: opponents, line-up and chance all play a part.
