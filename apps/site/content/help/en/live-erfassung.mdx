On "Matchday" you tap what happens from the side of the court. You only need this if there is no official match report with the goals.

## How it works

Open the match and choose "Matchday". Big buttons for "Our goal", "Their goal", "Good play", "Mistake" and "Watch later". Small ones for 2 minutes, time-out and half-time. The match clock runs along. You can fix a mistake with "undo".

## No network

Matchday works offline. Everything is saved on the device and sent as soon as there is a network again.

## Someone else taps

With "Share" you hand Matchday to someone on the bench, without an account.

## With video

If you upload the video later, your entries are linked to the video by match time.
