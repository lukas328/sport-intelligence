With live tagging you tap events into your phone or tablet directly courtside.

## How it works

Open the match and choose "Live". Large buttons for goal, missed shot, save, turnover, penalty and timeout; you tap the shooting position on the court. The match clock runs along.

## Without a connection

Tagging works offline. Everything is stored on the device and synchronised as soon as a connection is available again.

## Combining with video

If a video is uploaded later, the live-tagged events are matched to the video via the match time. Tagged events take precedence over automatically detected ones.
