Many clubs already film their matches, often with a parent's phone in the stands. That is enough for an automatic analysis, as long as a few things are right.

## 1. The camera does not move

A tripod is the most important accessory. The analysis converts pixels into court positions and needs a steady image to do so. Panning and zooming make this difficult.

## 2. High up, from the centre

The higher, the better: from about 3 metres up, players hide each other less. The centre line is the best spot, because both goals are equally visible.

## 3. The whole court in the frame

Both goal areas and both sidelines must be in the picture. Better a bit more margin than a corner cut off.

## 4. 1080p and enough battery

Full HD is enough, 50 frames per second help with fast plays. Turn on flight mode, turn off the screen lock and bring a power bank: with breaks, a match lasts around 90 minutes.

## 5. Everyone is informed

Let the opponent and the hall know that you are filming. A notice such as "This match is being filmed for analysis purposes" makes things clear. You will find a template in the [recording guide](../aufnahme).

More details are in the help article [Filming a match](../hilfe/aufnahme).
