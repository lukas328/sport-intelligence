An automatic video analysis is not always right. Players hide each other, the light dazzles, the ball is small and fast. The question is not whether mistakes happen, but how you deal with them.

## Every event has a source

In Handball Intelligence you can see for every event where it comes from: detected automatically, tagged live or corrected by you. Automatically detected events carry a confidence level. If it is low, the event is flagged and should be checked.

## Estimates are called estimates

Paths, shot positions and running distances from a single camera are estimates. We draw them dashed. We say so, instead of pretending to a precision that does not exist.

## What we detect today and what not yet

The first version detects players, teams, phases of possession, throw-offs and the goals derived from them. Shots, saves and turnovers you still record yourself today, live courtside or afterwards in the video. We test every new detection on real matches before it creates events automatically.

## You have the final say

Every event can be changed. Corrections feed into the key figures immediately, and insights only appear when the sample is large enough. That way, the figures you show in the video session remain figures you can stand behind.
