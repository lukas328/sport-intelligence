An automatic video analysis sees a lot, but not everything equally well. Players hide each other, the light dazzles, the ball is small and fast. That is why, with us, the machine does not decide alone what happened in the match. You do.

## Every event has a source

In Handball Intelligence you see for every event where it comes from: from the video, from the match report, recorded live or corrected by you. When the analysis is unsure about a moment, it asks you instead of hiding it.

## A few questions instead of goal by goal

The analysis finds the court, follows the players and the ball, and suggests the moments when a goal was scored. Where it is unsure, it asks you while checking: at most ten questions per match, usually answered in a few minutes. When that is enough for you, you put the rest aside with a single tap. You confirm the court with a single tap, and if the camera stands the same way in the same hall again, the analysis takes it over for the next video. Where a goal was thrown from, you tap if you like; that is what the three things are made of.

## Every number with its base

"9 of 18 goals conceded on the right wing" says more than "50 per cent": you see at once what the number rests on. And every number has its scenes, so you see for yourself what is behind it.

## You have the last word

Every event can be changed. Corrections go straight into the figures, and findings only appear when enough goals stand behind them. So the numbers you show in the video session stay numbers you can stand behind.
