## Measurements
- Live mark sync (synthetic, `packages/metrics/tests/test_sync.py`): 200 matches with 30 to 60
  goals, taps 0.5 to 4 s after the goal for 85 % of goals, 70 % of goals detected in the video with
  1.5 s noise plus false detections, camera clock off by up to 2 minutes (no clock in 20 %):
  **200 of 200 within 2 s** (median error 0.33 s, 90th percentile 0.71 s). Target: 90 % within 2 s.
  Real matches: pending (needs footage and a coach tapping, Q-ADD-8).
- Paper sheets: the acceptance (95 % of number fields on 10 real sheets) needs the sheets and a
  model key; `pytest -m live apps/api/tests/test_paper_vision.py` runs it once photos and true
  values are in `ml/eval/paper_sheets` (Q-M11-1).
- App: cold start under 2 s and a 70-minute recording need a device build (Q-M11-2).

## Vision-Check
- P1 Automatisch zuerst: eingehalten. Marks sync themselves after the analysis; without marks
  nothing is missing. Paper sheets are read automatically, the user only confirms.
- P2 Jede Zahl führt zum Video: eingehalten. Synced marks and app scenes jump to the video;
  federation figures are aggregates on purpose (no video for the federation).
- P3 Ehrlich statt beeindruckend: eingehalten. Uncertain readings and failed checks are marked, the
  sync shows its source (goals, camera clock, by hand), measurements say "synthetic" where they are.
- P4 Amateur zuerst: eingehalten. A phone as camera with storage, battery and level checks; marks
  offline; parents reply in the app.
- P5 Team-Plattform, Torhüter als Modul: nicht betroffen.
- P6 Nachwuchs schützen: eingehalten. No public profiles in youth teams, minors only with a parent,
  consent checked on every request; push texts name nobody.
- P7 Datenschutz als Standard: eingehalten. Profiles not indexed by default, federations see no club
  content and aggregates only from five clubs, signed media links expire after four hours, tokens in
  the keychain.
- P8 Handball-Sprache: eingehalten. App, sheets and marks in de-CH, fr, en (Tor wir, Gegentor,
  7 m, Halbzeit).
- P9 Partner statt alles selbst: eingehalten. Expo push for APNs/FCM, existing storage multipart
  upload, federation billing on the existing QR invoice.
- P10 Schnell und günstig: eingehalten. Sync is a pure computation, push and invoices run on the
  existing worker and PDF code, no new servers.
